# Optional practical examination (90 minutes)

Outside the 12 contact hours. Use an isolated VM and instructor-supplied fixtures. No Internet access. Each task 25 points; 20 minutes each, plus 10 minutes to submit.

1. Analyze an unseen PCAP: identify protocols, annotate a connection and justify a security observation.
2. Diagnose a TLS validation failure, correct the configuration and demonstrate successful authenticated connection.
3. Diagnose a firewall policy discrepancy and show both positive and negative tests.
4. Interpret IDS alerts, match them to labelled flows and calculate precision, recall and false-positive rate.

Submit short answers, commands, evidence and corrected artifacts. Instructor must prepare fresh variants; do not reuse answers from the training datasets. Award 10 points for diagnosis, 10 for implementation/analysis and 5 for evidence and explanation per task.

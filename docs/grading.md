Detailed rubrics in each lab take precedence. For each criterion award 100% of points for correct, supported and reproducible work; 75% for minor omissions; 50% for partial evidence; 25% for minimal credible progress; 0% for absent or incorrect evidence. Interpolate where justified. Overall lab grade = (G01+G02+G03+G04)/4. Suggested bands: 90–100 excellent, 80–89 very good, 70–79 good, 60–69 satisfactory, 50–59 partial, below 50 insufficient. Official pass threshold is set by the institution.

## Experimental standards

Record versions, dataset hashes, configuration and topology. Use positive and negative controls, baseline measurements, fixed conditions, raw results, at least 30 independent latency samples and 5 throughput runs where specified. Report mean, median, sample SD and 95% t interval where independent samples and distribution assumptions are reasonable; otherwise discuss limitations. A confidence interval is not a guarantee. Do not infer causation from an uncontrolled comparison. Undefined ratios are N/A, not zero. For IDS use flow-level labels and independent calibration/test sets.
